# AERIS Indonesia
**Made By Zarvions_Z**

Explore Indonesia from Aceh to Papua, choose your aircraft, and take command of your flight.

## 1. Choose your mode

- **Free Play:** Explore airports and aircraft freely.
- **Career Play:** Complete departures and earn XP. Progress saves automatically in your browser.

## 2. Prepare your flight

1. Choose your departure airport.
2. Click **Change** to select an aircraft and airline-inspired livery.
3. Select **Gate → taxi → takeoff**, **Discovery flight**, or **Landing practice**.
4. Choose your weather.
5. Click **Start flight**.

Discovery flights start airborne. Landing practice begins on approach.

## 3. Depart from the gate

Follow the **Ground Operations / ATC** panel:

1. Board passengers.
2. Request pushback.
3. Follow the guided taxi sequence.
4. Obtain takeoff clearance.
5. Hold **Shift** to increase throttle.
6. At your aircraft's indicated rotation speed, gently hold **↓** or **S** to raise the nose.
7. Once airborne, press **G** to retract the landing gear.

## 4. Flight controls

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Pitch up / down | **↓ / ↑** or **S / W** |
| Bank left / right | **← / →** or **A / D** |
| Rudder left / right | **Q / E** |
| Increase / decrease throttle | **Shift / Ctrl** |
| Wheel brakes | Hold **Space** |
| Landing gear | **G** |
| Level assist | **L** |
| Cockpit / chase view | **C** |
| Indonesia map | **M** |
| Pause / resume | **P** |
| Restart flight | **R** |
| Flight controls guide | **H** |

Use gentle inputs. Watch your airspeed, altitude, and warning messages.

## 5. Move the chase camera

Press **C** to select Chase view.

- **Hold left-click and drag** over the scenery to orbit around your aircraft.
- Release the mouse to keep that viewing angle.
- **Double-click the scenery** to reset behind the aircraft.

Dragging the camera does not steer the plane.

## 6. Advanced aircraft controls

Press **K** to open the searchable keyboard guide.

To operate a system, press **X**, then its two-digit number. For example, **X → 2 → 5** toggles the APU.

| System | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Engines | **I** |
| APU | **U** |
| Electrical power | **V** |
| Anti-ice | **J** |
| Landing gear | **G** |
| Level assist | **L** |

All **47 system commands** appear in the guide. Some actions have restrictions—for example, landing gear cannot retract while the aircraft is on the ground.

## 7. Land your aircraft

1. Align with the runway.
2. Reduce throttle and approach at your aircraft's suggested speed.
3. Ensure the landing gear is down; press **G** if it needs lowering.
4. Keep your wings level and descend gently.
5. After touchdown, reduce throttle to zero and hold **Space** to brake.

## 8. Redeem a code

1. Click **Redeem** in the top menu.
2. Enter your code.
3. Click **Redeem** to reveal the reward.
4. Press **Escape** or click **Close** to return.

If “Reward image unavailable” appears, the reward image has not been added to that version of the game.

## Tips

- Manual pitch or bank inputs disengage level assist.
- Opening a guide or menu pauses the flight simulation.
- Career progress stays in the browser and website where you earned it.
- AERIS is a simplified flight game; aircraft handling, systems, and airport scenery are designed for gameplay.
